How it works

The cover is embedded with a CLIP vision model and matched against a reference index of comic covers using nearest-neighbor search (FAISS). No training — a new comic is added just by embedding its cover. Best results when the cover you photograph resembles one of the ~20 graphic novels in the demo index.
